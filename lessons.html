What's covered

Built around your ear, not a syllabus

Technique, groove, reading (standard notation, tab and charts), theory as it applies to real parts, and — once you're ready — improvisation and songwriting. Guitar lessons follow the same philosophy, with extra focus on rhythm playing and how it locks in with bass.

Every student gets a short practice plan after each lesson. No generic method book — the material comes from songs you actually want to play.
